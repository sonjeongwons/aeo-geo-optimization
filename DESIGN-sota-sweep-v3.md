## Rejected (correctly)
- **Full cited-source corpus + co-citation graph** — duplicate of v2 critic #1; and its headline input (groundingChunks) was vaporware until V1. Revive as the v2 earned-source build ONCE V1 ships, with a URL-shape guard + Wilson CIs (never a bare "co-cited 32%").
- **Video-modality citation channel + segment back-attribution** — duplicate of v2 critic #1/#5; relied on non-existent intent strata (how-to/healthcare) and a §0-risky transcript-fetch + over-certain timestamp back-attribution.

## Build order
**V1 (grounding) is the keystone** — it produces the retrieval trace the earned-source corpus, fan-out coverage, citation-fidelity (V3), and video-modality builds all depend on. Then: V6 (cheap pure gate) → V2 (sampling cost) → V4 (sentiment volatility) → V5 (embedding proximity, adds embed() seam) → V7 (multi-turn, XL, after R4). Once V1 is live with real grounding data, BUILD the v2 earned-source-corpus entry (no longer vaporware).
